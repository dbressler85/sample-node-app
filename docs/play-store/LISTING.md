## Full description (max 4000)

```
Dynasty Central is the command center for fantasy managers who run more than one
MyFantasyLeague dynasty league. Instead of jumping between league sites, see every team,
every deadline, and every decision in one place — and act on them without leaving the app.

WHAT YOU GET

• Cross-league home — every league's status at a glance: who's on the clock, whose lineup
  needs setting, imminent waivers, and pending trade offers, sorted by what's most urgent.

• Lineups — a scoring- and format-aware optimizer with floor/ceiling projections, injury and
  bye awareness, and a one-tap "review and set" that never files a change you didn't see.

• Waivers — FAAB and priority (FCFS) boards, suggested bids, and a Waiver Wizard that lines
  up pickups across all your leagues at once. Won and outbid claims both surface.

• Trades — a full-deal suggester built from team needs and fair value, a live trade block,
  and an inbox to accept, reject, or withdraw offers.

• Draft — a live draft board (make your pick in-app), a rankings-driven draft list for slow
  and startup drafts, and your future-pick inventory across every league.

• Players — cross-league rankings, best-available free agents, a searchable player hub with
  news and prior-year stats, a compare tool, and a watchlist that tracks a player everywhere
  you could roster him.

• Portfolio — your total dynasty value with trend history, league-outlook mix, and
  cross-league value arbitrage so you can see where you're rich and where you're thin.

• Notifications — get told the moment you're on the clock, when a trade offer lands, when a
  waiver claim resolves, or when a starting lineup needs attention.

Values are market-anchored (FantasyCalc) and format-aware — 1QB or Superflex, TE-premium,
league size — so the numbers reflect YOUR leagues, not a generic redraft board. Player news is
powered by RotoBaller.

REQUIRES a MyFantasyLeague account. Your MFL password is only used to sign you in to
MyFantasyLeague — it is never stored by Dynasty Central.

Dynasty Central is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored
by MyFantasyLeague, FantasyCalc, Sleeper, or RotoBaller. All league data is your own, retrieved
from MyFantasyLeague on your behalf.
```
