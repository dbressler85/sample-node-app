Dynasty Central ("the app", "we", "us") is an independent companion app for managing
MyFantasyLeague dynasty leagues. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. The
app is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by** MyFantasyLeague, FantasyCalc, or
Sleeper.

## What we handle, and why

**Your MyFantasyLeague login.** To use the app you sign in with your MyFantasyLeague (MFL)
username and password. Your **password is used only to authenticate you with MyFantasyLeague
and is never stored** by the app or its backend. Once you're signed in, we keep only the
resulting **MFL session cookie** so you stay logged in; it is encrypted at rest and is cleared
when you log out or it expires.

**Your MFL username.** Retained with your session to identify you and to organize your leagues
while you're signed in.

**Your in-app actions.** Content you create in the app — player tags (target/avoid), your
watchlist, draft lists, trade blocks, and waiver claims — is stored to provide those features.
Waiver claims and trade actions are sent to MyFantasyLeague on your behalf.

**Push-notification token.** If you enable notifications, we store a push token for your device
so we can deliver alerts you opt into (on-the-clock draft picks, trade offers, waiver results,
lineup reminders). The token is passed to Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging and Expo's push
service **only to route those notifications** — never for advertising or analytics. You can turn
notifications off in Settings or your device settings at any time.

**League and player data.** The app retrieves your league, roster, and player data from
MyFantasyLeague on your behalf, and shows market player values from public sources
(FantasyCalc, Sleeper). We do not send your personal data to those value sources.

## What we do NOT do

- No advertising, and no advertising or marketing identifiers.
- No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No collection of location, contacts, calendar, photos/media, microphone, health, financial,
  or messaging data.
- No sale of your data to anyone.

## Security

All communication between the app, our backend, and MyFantasyLeague uses encrypted transport
(HTTPS). The stored MFL session cookie is encrypted at rest.

## Data retention & deletion

The MFL session cookie is kept only while you're signed in and is removed on logout. To request
deletion of any data associated with your account, contact **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** and we will
delete it.

## Children

The app is intended for fantasy-football managers and is not directed to children under 13, and
we do not knowingly collect data from them.
